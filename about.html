RipWatch tracks card pack pulls and buybacks live, using only public blockchain data. No accounts, no guesses: just what the chain shows.

What the data showed

Snapshot, Nov 12 to 16, 2026, about 121,000 pulls.

  • Packs paid back about 80 cents per dollar on average when the card was sold straight back. That is a house edge of roughly 15 to 23 percent, similar on cheap and expensive packs.
  • 62% of pulls were sold back within one minute. 83% were sold back overall.
  • The top 10% of wallets opened about half of all packs.
  • About $6.8 million was paid out in buybacks in 77 hours. The median buyback was about $22.
  • Pack prices follow a pattern: the minimum value is about 70% of the price and the maximum is 16x the price (Spicy packs: 50% and 32x), based on the min and max values shown on the packs.
  • No hour of the day gave better odds.

How to read these numbers

  • Only cards that were sold back have a dollar value. Cards people kept are not counted, so true returns could be somewhat higher.
  • About a third of pulls had no pack name on-chain, so pack-level numbers are incomplete, especially for the biggest hits.
  • Wallet addresses are shortened.
